How to type sizes
  • Type sizes as feet.inches: 3.6 = 3 ft 6 in, 16.5 = 16 ft 5 in, 0.8 = 8 in.
  • Fill any two of Length, Width, Height to get area (sq ft). Fill all three to get volume (cu ft).
  • Enter jumps to the next box and adds a new row after the last one. Ctrl+S saves.
